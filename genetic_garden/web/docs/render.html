Rendering

Turning a skeleton into SVG, at a size it will actually be seen at. Most of this module is about what not to draw.

source: genetic_garden/render.py · every figure on this page is drawn by the code it describes

1The layer stack

A plant is stacked like a flat illustration: a band of foliage, then wood, then more foliage, so limbs and leaves interleave instead of every twig sitting on top of the trunk.

Within the branch layer the skeleton's own emit order is preserved — that is where the junction behaviour lives. Every other layer is flat.

2Line weights in output pixels

This is the single idea the module is built around. Line weights are specified in output pixels and converted into plant space once the fit scale is known.

That one trick lets the same plant render as a 512px illustration and a 20px list icon without the twigs vanishing or the outlines turning to mud.

3Drop, do not clamp

the rule

Structure below a couple of pixels is dropped, not clamped up to a visible width.

Clamping is the obvious thing and it is wrong. A mature tree at 16px has hundreds of twigs; clamp each to a minimum visible width and you get a ball of fat sticks that reads as nothing at all. Drop them and what remains is a trunk, a few primary limbs and the canopy masses — which is exactly what a tree looks like at 16px.

The same principle applies to twigs buried inside the crown at any size: they are invisible, and they were costing a third of the file.

4Where the bytes go

Every optimisation here came from measurement, and the measurements were surprising more than once.

  • Grouped styles. Almost every path shares its fill and stroke with dozens of others. Paths are bucketed by (layer, style) and emitted inside one <g> per bucket — roughly half the output was repeated attributes.
  • Precision follows scale. There is no point writing three decimals of a coordinate that lands inside one pixel.
  • Branch highlights. These turned out to cost more than the branches themselves — a 40% opacity highlight being drawn on five-pixel twigs where nothing could possibly see it.
